🛠️ How-To Guides

Practical tutorials for smarter recycling at home and on the go.

Recycle Plastic Right

Check the resin code, rinse containers, and keep caps on if accepted. When in doubt, leave it out — wishcycling hurts more than it helps.

Sort Kitchen Waste

Use separate bins for recycling, compost, and trash. Label them clearly and empty the recycling stream before food dries on containers.

Handle E-Waste Safely

Remove batteries when possible, wipe personal data, and drop off at certified events or HHW facilities — never in curbside bins.

Donate or Textile Recycle

Wearable clothes belong at donation centers. Damaged textiles go to textile recycling bins so fibers can be reused instead of landfilled.

Set Up a Home Station

Place a small kitchen caddy for scraps, a larger sorting bin in the garage, and a cheat sheet of local dos and don’ts on the fridge.

Read Local Rules

Every city is different. Bookmark your hauler’s accepted-materials list and check holiday schedule changes before setting out bins.
