📚 Step-by-Step Recycling Guides

Practical recycling tips and step-by-step guides to keep materials out of landfill.
♻️ Know Your Bins ▼
Learn the basics of recycling, compost, trash, and hazardous waste so you can sort with confidence.
🧼 Prep Recyclables ▼
Rinse containers, remove caps when needed, and flatten boxes to improve processing quality.
🥡 Avoid Common Mistakes ▼
Bags, greasy paper, and broken glass are common contaminants. Learn what belongs where.
🏠 Start at Home ▼
Simple sorting habits and a small bin setup make household recycling easier and more consistent.
